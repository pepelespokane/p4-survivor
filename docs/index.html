How it works

  • Every week you pick one team from each of the four Power 4 conferences: ACC, Big Ten, Big 12, SEC. Four picks a week.
  • No team can be used twice all season. 13 weeks, 52 picks, out of 67 P4 teams (ACC 17, Big Ten 18, Big 12 16, SEC 16). In the Big 12 and the SEC you burn 13 of 16, and most teams have a bye somewhere in the window, so the last few weeks get tight. Think ahead.
  • A pick locks when that team kicks off, and stays hidden from everyone else until then. You can always see your own.
  • Scoring is one point per correct pick, 52 possible. Ties break on fewest misses.
  • Teams on a bye cannot be picked. Neither can a team whose game has started.

Schedule, kickoff times and results come from ESPN and refresh automatically through the weekend. Scores can lag a live finish by a few minutes.
