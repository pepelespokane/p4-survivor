How it works

  • Every week you pick one team from each of the four Power 4 conferences: ACC, Big Ten, Big 12, SEC. Four picks a week.
  • No team can be used twice all season. 13 weeks, 52 picks, out of 67 P4 teams (ACC 17, Big Ten 18, Big 12 16, SEC 16). In the Big 12 and the SEC you burn 13 of 16, and most teams have a bye somewhere in the window, so the last few weeks get tight. Think ahead.
  • Each conference is its own survivor pool. Lose your SEC pick and your SEC run is over, but you are still alive in the ACC, Big Ten and Big 12. Four separate races, run at the same time.
  • Skipping a league ends your run in it. No pick submitted for a conference counts as a loss there once that week's games are final.
  • Each league has its own winner. Last one standing in a conference wins that conference. Four winners, one per league.
  • If nobody survives all 13 weeks in a league, whoever lasted longest in it wins it. Everyone knocked out of that league in the same week ties.
  • Change your picks as often as you like until kickoff. Once the team you picked kicks off, that league is locked for the week and cannot be switched, even to a team playing later.
  • Your picks stay hidden from everyone else until each team kicks off. You can always see your own.
  • Teams on a bye cannot be picked. Neither can a team whose game has started.
  • Once you are out of a league you can keep picking in it for bragging rights. It has no effect on the standings.

Schedule, kickoff times and results come from ESPN and refresh automatically through the weekend. Scores can lag a live finish by a few minutes.
